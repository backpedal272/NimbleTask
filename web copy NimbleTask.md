# NimbleTask

### Your to-do list is just text. Finally.

NimbleTask is a tiny Mac app for people who'd rather *type* their tasks than click through forms, drag cards around, or learn a system that needs its own system.

Open a file. Write your list. Done. (Well, your list isn't done. But you know what we mean.)

---

## As simple as a sticky note

No databases, no accounts, no sync subscription, no onboarding carousel. Your tasks live in a plain text file you can open anywhere, back up anywhere, and still read in 20 years.

```
* Work
Website Redesign:
- Pick a font that isn't Comic Sans @design
- Reply to Marcus about the budget @email @waiting

* Home
Apartment:
- Call the plumber (again) @today
```

That's the whole format:

- **`* Work`** is an area, a big bucket for your life.
- **`Project:`** (ends with a colon) is a project.
- **`- Something`** is a task.
- **`@anything`** is a tag. Make up as many as you like.

If you already use TaskPaper files, NimbleTask opens them as they are.

---

## Find anything in a blink

Every tag and project gets its own little pill at the top of the window. Click one and everything else disappears.

- **Tag pills** show what's `@urgent`, `@waiting` or `@errand`. Combine them to narrow things down.
- **Project pills** show one project at a time.
- **Search** filters as you type.

The best part: **you can keep editing while filtered.** Fix a typo, add a task, tick something off, all without leaving the view. New tasks you add pick up the tags you're filtering by, so they don't vanish.

Press **Esc** and you're back to the full list.

---

## Keyboard-friendly, mouse-optional

| Shortcut | What it does |
|---|---|
| **⌘D** | Mark a task `@done` (or undo it) |
| **⌘T** | Flag a task for `@today` |
| **⌘F** | Jump to search |
| **Esc** | Clear all filters |
| **⌘Z** | Undo anything. Seriously, anything. |

---

## The small things, done right

- **Picks up where you left off.** Quit the app and the same files open again next time.
- **As many windows as you like.** Work list in one, grocery list in another.
- **A proper Mac app.** Native, fast, light, and it behaves the way your Mac expects.
- **Your files stay yours.** Keep them in iCloud Drive, Dropbox or any folder. No lock-in.

---

## Who's it for?

People who like plain text, think in lists, and want a to-do app that stays out of the way.

**NimbleTask. Write it down, then go do it.**
